<h1 class="page-title">Privacy policy</h1>
<p class="lede">This policy covers digitalyogi.tech, KB (kb.digitalyogi.tech) and DigitalYogi Learn (learn.digitalyogi.tech), run by DigitalYogi Lab (OPC) Private Limited, Bengaluru, India ("DigitalYogi", "we").</p>

<h2>What we collect</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Your Google account basics</b>, when you choose "Continue with Google": your name, email address and profile picture. We use them to sign you in and to match your purchases to your account.</li>
<li><b>Your email address</b>, if you sign in to Learn with an emailed code instead.</li>
<li><b>Billing details</b> you enter before a paid purchase: name, address, city, state, PIN code and, optionally, GSTIN. We keep them with your order to issue invoices and meet tax rules.</li>
<li><b>Orders and course progress</b> on Learn: what you bought or enrolled in, and which lessons you completed.</li>
<li><b>An optional WhatsApp number</b>, only if you add one on your Learn account page, to tell you about new lessons.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What stays in your own Google Drive</h2>
<p>When you sign in to KB with Google, your KB work (answers, notes, AI feedback, tutor chats, settings and the AI key you choose to save) is stored in a private, app-only area of <b>your</b> Google Drive. KB asks Google only for access to that area (the <code>drive.appdata</code> permission). It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files, and we do not copy this data to our servers.</p>

<h2>AI providers</h2>
<p>KB's AI grading and tutor use an AI provider you choose (for example Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI or xAI Grok) with your own API key. Your answer and the question are sent from your browser straight to that provider and are handled under its terms. We do not receive them.</p>

<h2>Payments</h2>
<p>Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details; Razorpay shares with us only the payment's status and reference.</p>

<h2>Services we rely on</h2>
<p>Supabase (accounts, orders and progress for Learn), Cloudflare (hosting), Bunny.net (course video), Razorpay (payments), Google (sign-in and Drive storage) and our email provider (sign-in codes and receipts). Each processes data only to provide its service.</p>

<h2>What we don't do</h2>
<p>We don't sell your personal data, and we don't use advertising trackers on KB or Learn. We may use your email to send receipts and important account messages.</p>

<h2>Keeping and deleting your data</h2>
<p>We keep order and billing records as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your account and any other data at any time. You can delete your KB data yourself: remove KB's access in your Google account settings (Security → Third-party connections), which also deletes its app-only Drive data.</p>

<h2>Google user data</h2>
<p>DigitalYogi's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the <a href="https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy">Google API Services User Data Policy</a>, including the Limited Use requirements.</p>
